4. GET QUANTITIES AND NUTRITION RIGHT

These conventions are mandatory:
- ingredients quantities are for the WHOLE recipe at recipe.servings.
- nutritionPerServing is ALREADY per serving; never divide it by servings again.
- Substitution quantities, possibleAddOns, and possibleRemovals are whole-recipe quantities.
- scalingNotes.*.changes contains PER-SERVING signed deltas against the original base.
- Optional ingredients and add-ons are excluded from base nutrition until selected.

For a requested preparation count S and original servings B:
scaledBaseQuantity = originalQuantity * S / B.
For one authored scaling change d:
adjustedQuantity = scaledBaseQuantity + d * S.
For a selected substitute with source alternative quantity A:
replacementQuantity = A * S / B; remove the original ingredient rather than adding both.

Distinguish servings PREPARED from servings EATEN. Changing prepared servings changes ingredient demand and whole-batch totals, not nutrition per serving. Planner nutrition equals the chosen variant's nutrition per serving multiplied by servings eaten.

Recalculate changed recipes from final ingredient quantities and each ingredient's nutritionReference.basisQuantity/basisUnit. Preserve the dataset's available-carbohydrate convention and calorie references. Sum before rounding; use the documented half-up rounding: whole kcal and 0.1 g macros. Do not add already-rounded nutrition endpoints together.

Never silently convert g to ml, raw to cooked weight, dry to cooked grain, drained weight to package weight, or prepared stock to concentrate. Never scale cooking time linearly with serving count. totalTimeMinutes includes mandatory resting/chilling, so overnight oats must not appear as a finished 10-minute meal.
